This thesis presented FL-ADTCN, the first framework to couple a single metaheuristic optimisation algorithm — DB-BOA — to three simultaneously active roles in a real Hyperledger Fabric consortium blockchain: hyperparameter optimisation of the ADTCN fraud detection model (Job~1), leader node selection for consensus efficiency (Job~2), and federated aggregation weight determination (Job~3), with all three objectives coupled through an on-chain economic incentive mechanism.

The framework resolves the three fundamental barriers to collaborative financial fraud detection. Privacy is guaranteed by federated learning — raw transaction data never crosses institutional boundaries. Trust is guaranteed by Hyperledger Fabric — incentive rules are encoded in chaincode that no single organisation controls and that the immutable ledger cannot subsequently alter. Incentive alignment is guaranteed by the coupling between DB-BOA Job~3 output and token reward distribution — organisations that contribute better models earn more, automatically and verifiably.

Empirical results demonstrate that the FL-ADTCN achieves 97.38\% accuracy and MCC of 0.966 under federated conditions — surpassing the original DB-BOA-ADTCN base paper across all metrics, including its reported 95.45\% accuracy under non-federated centralised conditions. Blockchain throughput sustains 85 TPS with average 180\,ms latency. DB-BOA Job~2 reduces consensus latency by 28.4\% relative to random leader selection. Byzantine resilience testing confirms that the incentive mechanism automatically isolates a malicious organisation within 12 rounds without any human intervention.

The primary novel contribution — DB-BOA for federated aggregation weight determination on a live blockchain ledger — is the first instance in the literature of a metaheuristic algorithm providing mathematically derived (not heuristic) federated reward distribution. This qualitative distinction between mathematical derivation and heuristic allocation is the central architectural advance that this thesis contributes to the field of blockchain-integrated federated machine learning.

Future work will focus on differential privacy integration, quantum-resistant cryptographic upgrades, asynchronous federation protocols, and live deployment validation across real banking infrastructure. The codebase, including the Hyperledger Fabric network configuration, DBBOAContract chaincode, DB-BOA Python implementation, and FL-ADTCN model, is publicly available at \url{https://github.com/shadmanshafin49/DB-BOA-FEL-ADTCN-Hyperledger-Fabric}.
