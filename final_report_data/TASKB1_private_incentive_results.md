**Headline.** Moving the incentive signal off the ~111,874-dim weight channel onto an output-perturbation channel on the 3-dim contribution vector φ improves the privacy budget at which on-chain rewards stay rank-faithful from **ε\*=3000.0 (weight)** to **ε\*=50.0 (output)** — a **60×** improvement, the same order of magnitude as the √(d/n)≈193× *upper bound* predicted by noise/signal ∝ k·√(dim)/ε. (The 60× realised vs 193× bound gap is expected: ε\* is read off discrete sweep points and is set by the finite inter-org signal gaps, not by the worst-case dimensional bound — so 193× is a ceiling, not a target.) The negative privacy↔incentive result is thus a property of the *channel*, not of DP-plus-Shapley per se.

**Ground truth (ε=∞):** weights [0.619, 0.25, 0.131], tokens [12.38, 5.0, 2.62].
