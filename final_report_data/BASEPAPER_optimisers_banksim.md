### Three caveats that decide how this table may be read

**1. Equal population and iterations is NOT equal objective evaluations.** Each algorithm calls the fitness a different number of times per iteration by construction: MBO 90, WSA 166, DBOA 167, BOA 170, DB-BOA 124. That is a 1.9x range. The budget is matched the way this literature matches it — population and iteration count — not by evaluation count, and the counts are printed above so the reader can judge rather than assume.

**2. The test-MCC differences are inside the noise.** The spread across all five optimisers is 0.0350, while the largest per-optimiser spread across seeds is 0.1160 — larger than the effect being claimed. On the metric that actually matters, **these optimisers are not distinguishable from one another.** DB-BOA-ADTCN has the best mean search fitness and WSA-ADTCN the best test MCC, and on this evidence neither lead is meaningful.

**3. Search wall-clock is confounded, not an efficiency measure.** The surrogate's cost scales with the filter count a candidate proposes, so an optimiser that explores wide-filter regions pays more per evaluation regardless of its own efficiency. Seconds-per-evaluation therefore varies several-fold across the table for reasons that have nothing to do with optimiser quality. It is reported for transparency and should not be quoted as a speed comparison — which is precisely what the base paper's Table 5 does.
